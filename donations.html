Support Casa Río

Casa Río is funded by people who want to help build a permanent cultural space in Madrid. Contributions are made through Stripe, an online payment platform designed to handle payments securely. Stripe processes the payment details required for the transaction; Casa Río does not receive or store your full card or bank details.

Each contribution goes into the Casa Río fund and helps move the project through its published phases. You can choose whether your contribution is recorded under your name, an alias or anonymously.

Contributions are voluntary and do not grant ownership of Casa Río, repayment rights or any financial return.

Contributions are not currently tax-deductible.

TRANSPARENCY

Every contribution and expense is recorded as the project develops. The accounts show what has come in, what has been spent and the current balance, creating a continuous public record of how the fund is used.
