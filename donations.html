Support Casa Río

Casa Río is funded by people who want to help build a permanent cultural space in Madrid. Contributions are made through Stripe. Payment information is processed directly by Stripe; Casa Río does not receive or store your card or bank details.

Transparency is part of the project. Casa Río publishes what comes in, what is spent and what remains. You can choose whether your contribution appears under your name, an alias or anonymously.

CONTRIBUTE THROUGH STRIPE →

Contributions are voluntary and do not grant ownership of Casa Río, repayment rights or any financial return.

Contributions are not currently tax-deductible.

TRANSPARENCY

You do not need to contribute before seeing how Casa Río manages its money. The accounts are part of the public record of the project.
