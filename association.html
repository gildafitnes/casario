1.2 — Create the association

Casa Río begins as a citizen-led initiative, but it is not intended to remain legally or financially dependent on one person.

One of the first milestones will therefore be to establish Casa Río as a non-profit association.

This gives Casa Río its own legal identity, NIF, bank account, accounting records and governing structure. From that point onwards, contributions can be held and managed directly by Casa Río rather than through an individual.

It also creates the legal structure needed to sign contracts, organise events, sell publications and artworks, employ people and, eventually, acquire and manage the permanent space.

What will it cost?

Casa Río will initially hire a professional gestoría to prepare the association correctly and handle the registration process, statutes, NIF and initial administrative setup.

Official registration fees: approximately €45–€80

Professional constitution and administrative service: approximately €150–€400

Estimated initial cost: €200–€500

Real quotations will be requested before contracting the service and published in Casa Río's financial archive.

Once the association has been established, Casa Río does not need to maintain a permanent gestoría simply in order to exist.

During the initial fundraising stage, when activity is limited mainly to receiving contributions and paying occasional project expenses, the basic accounts and documentation can be managed internally.

Casa Río will record every contribution and expense and maintain the accounting documentation required by the association.

In addition, we will voluntarily make this information public through the Casa Río financial archive.

This public transparency is not simply an administrative obligation. It is part of how Casa Río has chosen to operate.

Professional accounting, tax and employment services will be contracted again when Casa Río begins regular economic activity: workshops, sales, café or food activity, employees, invoices and other operations that require more complex administration.

Why become an association?

Because Casa Río should eventually belong to Casa Río.

The association separates the project from its founder and creates an organisation capable of holding its own money, employing people, entering into agreements and eventually owning the permanent space.

Being non-profit does not mean that nobody can be paid. It means that the money generated by Casa Río must remain within the project and be used to support its objectives.

The association is the point at which Casa Río begins to exist as an organisation of its own.
