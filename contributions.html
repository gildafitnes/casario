How are contributions managed?

Transparency is part of the infrastructure of Casa Río.

If the project is built with contributions from other people, those people should be able to see what happens to that money.

For this reason, Casa Río will maintain a public financial dashboard from the beginning of the project.

At any moment, you will be able to see:

Total received

Everything that has entered the Casa Río fund.

Total spent

Everything that has been used to build, operate or develop the project.

Available balance

The money currently remaining in the Casa Río fund.

Where the money went

Expenses organised by category, with supporting documentation whenever possible.

Recent contributions

The latest contributions received, with donors appearing by name, as Anonymous, or not publicly listed according to their preference.

The dashboard will also show how long Casa Río has been active and how close the project is to its next objective.

Current milestones

  • 02 — Initial fund
  • 03 — Association funded

  • 10 — Property acquired
  • 11 — Casa Río opens

Every completed milestone will remain documented in the archive.

The objective is not simply to publish an annual financial report after decisions have already been made.

The objective is to make the financial state of Casa Río visible while it is happening.

Budgets, invoices, quotations, reports and significant financial decisions will gradually form part of the same public archive.

Personal information, bank details and other legally protected data will be removed before publication.
