What happens if we cannot buy a space?

Casa Río does not begin with a building.

It begins with the activities, people and structures that can eventually make a permanent space possible.

If the fund is not yet sufficient, property prices rise, or an appropriate building cannot be found, Casa Río will remain in this stage for as long as necessary.

During that time, it will continue as an itinerant project, working with temporary and borrowed spaces throughout the city.

Casa Río can continue organising exhibitions, workshops, talks, performances, publications, collaborations and fundraising events. These activities allow the project to grow, become visible, build a community and generate additional resources while the search continues.

The financial objective remains the acquisition of a permanent space.

Part of Casa Río's work during this period is therefore directed towards making that acquisition increasingly possible: expanding the project, finding new sources of funding, researching the property market, building partnerships and communicating what Casa Río is trying to create.

Money contributed to Casa Río supports this wider process, not only the final property transaction.

If buying a space takes longer than expected, the project does not disappear.

Casa Río continues moving towards the purchase while existing, temporarily, wherever it can.

The building is a destination.

Casa Río can begin before reaching it.
