Casa Río is a citizen-led, non-profit initiative working to reclaim spaces in the city.

Spaces for culture, community and collective life, free from capitalist violence.

why this matters?

We are currently in the first phase of the project, raising funds to acquire a permanent space and return it to the city for culture, community and collective use.

Where does your donation go?

Once secured, the space will be transformed into a place where artists, neighbours and communities can meet, create, learn and organise without the pressure to consume.

What will happen inside?

Casa Río is not built around maximising profit. With the support of many people, the space can remain accessible, modest in scale and focused on what it gives back to the city rather than on how much it can extract from it.

Casa Río can only exist if enough of us decide that spaces like this should exist.

A small contribution, multiplied by many people, can turn an idea into a permanent place for the city.
