What will happen inside?

Casa Río is conceived first of all as a space for living together: for meeting, creating, listening, exchanging ideas and spending time without the obligation to consume. Its programme will grow from this principle rather than from a fixed cultural agenda.

The space could host exhibitions, talks, assemblies, workshops, independent book and edition sales, acoustic concerts and projects proposed by the people who use it. One of these is “Project Tomorrow”, a daily exhibition opening at 8pm and remaining accessible until 1pm the following day, conceived not only as an exhibition but as a recurring meeting point for artists, neighbours and visitors.

Casa Río also aims to develop residencies for artists, creators and thinkers, where invited residents are paid to live and work in the house for a period of time. Some activities will be initiated by Casa Río; others will emerge from the people around it. The intention is not to fill every hour with programming, but to create the conditions for things to happen.
